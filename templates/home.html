Capabilities Overview

Why ThirdEye Matters

ThirdEye bridges fragmented investigative workflows into a unified, reproducible computer-vision environment.

Unified Vision Workflows

Traditional forensic sketch work and computerized image processing often exist as disconnected silos. ThirdEye combines foundational computer-vision workflows into a cohesive pipeline:

  • Facial sketch construction from standardized anatomical components
  • Bidirectional photo-to-sketch transformation
  • Computer-generated facial reconstruction from composite sketches
  • Controlled appearance variations (age progression, facial hair, hairstyles, eyewear)
  • Biometric face recognition against authorized enrolled records
  • Standardized printable A4 public-information and notice posters

Methodological Precision & Ethics

ThirdEye is engineered with strict technical discipline. Algorithmic reconstructions and appearance variations are analytical aids designed to explore visual hypotheses—not claims of guaranteed identity or exact biological ground truth.

By maintaining clear source attribution across real photographs, composite sketches, reconstructions, and appearance variations, the system empowers investigators and authorized analysts with actionable visual insights while maintaining full audit transparency.
